Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about installing and playing music with kuchbhi.

How do I install kuchbhi on my Android phone?
Click the "Download kuchbhi.apk" button above. Once downloaded, open the file from your notifications or Downloads folder. If prompted, allow "Install from unknown sources" in your browser settings. The app will install cleanly in 5 seconds.
How do I install on my Car's Android Touchscreen stereo?
Download the "Car Android Display Edition" (18.4 MB). Copy the APK file to a USB pen drive, plug the pen drive into your car's USB port, open the File Manager app on your car screen, and tap the APK to install. It is specially compiled for 32-bit car processors (Woodman, Hypersonic, Droid, Allwinner).
Does music keep playing when screen is locked?
Yes! kuchbhi utilizes Android foreground media service and ExoPlayer native playlist architecture. Playback continues uninterrupted in the background, with full media controls (Previous, Play/Pause, Next) available on your lockscreen and notification drawer.
Are downloaded songs full length or 30-second previews?
All tracks in kuchbhi are 100% full-length songs (3 to 6+ minutes) in CD Master 320kbps audio quality. 30-second preview clips have been completely eliminated.
